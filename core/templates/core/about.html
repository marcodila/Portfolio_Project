The Full Picture

I'm Marco Di Laudo — a Canadian-born, Texas-based graduate student completing a dual MBA/MSIS degree at Baylor University (May 2026). My academic and professional work sits at the intersection of applied AI, quantitative finance, and business strategy.

After Baylor, I'll be joining Deloitte as a consultant, where I plan to apply the same problem-solving approach I've developed in graduate school: understand the business problem first, then choose the right tool — whether that's a LangChain agent, a scikit-learn model, or a well-structured spreadsheet.

Outside of work and school, I'm an avid golfer, follow macro/monetary policy closely, enjoy live music festivals, and maintain a personal investment portfolio with a focus on long-term equity and alternatives. I believe the best analysts are curious people who read broadly — not just within their domain.
